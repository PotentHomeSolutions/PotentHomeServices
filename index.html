A little clarity

Questions before you start?

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Tell us about the different projects you have in mind so we can discuss the scope, sequencing, and licensed partner involvement.

What does Healthy Living include?

Healthy Living brings water treatment and indoor air-quality options together. Explore carbon filtration, softeners, reverse osmosis, PFAS / PFOS reduction, water disinfection, AC UV lights, and portable air purification.

Who handles the installation?

Installation is offered with licensed partner. The responsible partner and the scope of work should be identified in your proposal before installation is scheduled.

Which areas do you serve?

Broward County and surrounding areas. Include your ZIP code when you reach out so we can confirm availability for your location.
